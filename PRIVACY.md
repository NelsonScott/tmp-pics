# Privacy

Tmp collects nothing. It has no network code, so nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

- Photos you take are stored inside the app, on your phone only, and are not included in iCloud or computer
  backups. The app deletes each one when its time is up. Deleting the app deletes them all.
- The app never reads your photo library. It only adds a photo to it when you tap Save to Photos, and iOS
  asks you first.
- Share hands one photo to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/NelsonScott/tmp-pics/issues
