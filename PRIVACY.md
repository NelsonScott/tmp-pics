# Privacy

Tmp collects nothing. It has no network code, so nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

- Photos you take are saved to your Photos library, in an album you pick (Tmp unless you change it).
- The app keeps a small list of the photos it took and when they expire, on your phone only. Deleting the
  app deletes that list. Your photos stay in Photos until you delete them.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/NelsonScott/tmp-pics/issues
